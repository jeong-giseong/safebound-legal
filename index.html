Privacy Policy & Location Data Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 | Developer: JG Soft (제이지소프트)

JG Soft values your privacy and minimizes data collection. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal location data with third-party advertisers.

1. Data We Collect

  • Profile & Circle Info: Display nickname and 6-digit Circle invite code.
  • Location & Device Telemetry: Real-time GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, speed) and battery percentage (%) to display member status and trigger Safe Place (geofence) arrival/departure alerts.
  • 3-Second Walkie-Talkie (PTT) Audio: Ephemeral voice clips transmitted to your Circle members and automatically deleted after delivery.

2. Zero-Retention Safe-Return Web Links

When you generate a "Safe-Return Live Share" web link, a temporary token is created that expires automatically after 30 minutes or upon arrival. Once expired, access to the live coordinates is permanently revoked.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Location history is retained for up to 2 hours for free users and up to 30 days for PRO subscribers, after which it is automatically purged. You may leave a Circle or request immediate data deletion at any time by contacting our support team below.
